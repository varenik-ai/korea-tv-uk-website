Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

This privacy policy describes what data the korea-tv.uk website collects and how it is used.

1. What we collect

Korea-TV.uk does not require registration and does not directly collect personal data. However, the following may be recorded automatically when you visit the site:

  • IP address (anonymised by analytics systems)
  • Browser type and operating system
  • Pages you view and the time of your visit
  • Visitor country (IP-based, no exact address)

2. Analytics and tracking

  • Google Analytics 4 (GA4) — aggregated traffic statistics. Data is sent to Google.
  • Google Cast — the cast-to-TV button: in Chromium-based browsers a Google script (gstatic.com) is loaded; casting starts only after you press the button.

3. Cookies and browser storage

The site uses cookies for analytics. Player settings (sound, volume, quality, cinema mode, recently watched channels) are kept only in your browser's localStorage and are not sent to a server. You can delete cookies and site data in your browser settings.

4. Third-party content

Korea-TV.uk is a channel guide and does not store video content. Streams come from third parties via HLS. When a stream plays, your browser may connect directly to the source server, which may mean your IP address is shared with that server. For some channels the playlists and video pass through Cloudflare servers.

4.1. For rights holders: removal of streams

Links to streams on this site are taken from publicly available sources on the internet. Korea-TV.uk does not store, record or upload video content and is not affiliated with any broadcaster. If you are a rights holder and believe that a link infringes your rights, send a request to dyaltd@gmail.com stating the channel (page address) and attach documents proving your rights to the content. The stream will be removed from the site after proof of rights ownership is provided.

5. Your rights

You may request information about data that may be associated with your visit, or ask for its deletion. Contact: dyaltd@gmail.com

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date of the latest update is shown at the top of the document.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, rights holder requests and other enquiries: dyaltd@gmail.com
