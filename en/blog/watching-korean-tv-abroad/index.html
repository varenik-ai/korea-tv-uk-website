How to Watch Korean TV for Free Abroad: A Complete Guide

Updated: September 2026

For Koreans living abroad, and for anyone interested in Korean broadcasting and culture, easy access to live Korean TV can matter a lot — whether it's for keeping up with the news, staying connected to the language, or just watching a familiar channel. Here's an overview of how it actually works.

Do you need a VPN?

Some Korean broadcasters' own official streaming services restrict access to domestic IP addresses, in which case a VPN would be required. The channels collected on korea-tv.uk, however, use public streams that are already accessible from abroad, so you can watch them directly without a VPN.

What devices work

All you need is a browser — desktop, laptop, smartphone (iOS or Android), or a smart TV with a browser all work. There's no app to install and no account to create; playback starts directly on the website. If you'd rather switch between channels on mobile more conveniently, there's also a Telegram channel, @Korea_TV_hub.

Which channels are available

korea-tv.uk organizes 31 channels into 7 categories:

  • International — Arirang TV, Arirang Radio
  • EBS family — EBS 1TV, EBS 2TV, EBS Kids and other educational channels
  • Public & Government — KTV, National Assembly TV, and others
  • Regional — MBC and SBS regional affiliates
  • Religious, business/shopping, and music/radio channels

Common issues and how to fix them

If a stream freezes or takes a while to load, it's usually not a geo-restriction — it's more likely a temporary issue on the broadcaster's own streaming server, or your own connection. The player is designed to automatically try to reconnect in these situations; if nothing loads after about a minute, refreshing the page is usually the quickest fix.
